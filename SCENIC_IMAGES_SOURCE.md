# Scenic wallpapers: source and rights

The wallpapers `scenic_01` to `scenic_07` and `nightcar_18` were added on 16 November 2026 at the app owner's request, from one
shared ChatGPT image (a 3 x 3 collage titled "Nine Scenic Wallpaper Collage", 1024 x 1536 px). The pictures were generated by the
owner in ChatGPT, so the owner holds the rights under OpenAI's terms for generated output.

Each panel of the collage is only about 340 x 510 px. They were cut out, centre-cropped to 9:16 (the car was fitted to the
full width over a blurred copy of itself, like the other cars) and enlarged to 1080 x 1920 with a light sharpen, so they look softer
than the other wallpapers on a large screen. Replace any of them with a higher resolution original when one is available.

Left out: panel 2, 2 (a line drawing of an anime character from a copyrighted series, with Japanese text). Published characters

the website (see NIGHT_CARS_SOURCE.md). The `scenic_*` pictures are on the website and shown on the admin dashboard as
"AI-generated (yours)".
