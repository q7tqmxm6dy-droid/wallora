# Night car wallpapers: source and rights

The 17 `nightcar_*` wallpapers (Cars category) were added at the app owner's request from the local folder
`Wallpaper App Prompt_files` (images saved from a web page). **Their origin and licence were not verified.**
They look like AI-generated renders, and several show recognisable car-brand badges or grilles.

Before publishing to Google Play, confirm you have the right to use each image. If you cannot, delete the
rows below from `WallpaperCatalog.kt`, delete the files, and add their ids to `Taxonomy.retiredIds`.

Handling: each source image (about 1024 px) was fitted to the full width of a 1080 x 1920 canvas over a blurred,
darkened copy of itself. Titles are brand-neutral on purpose. Skipped for visible third-party text or livery,
or because they were too small: a game-franchise livery, a car with a tyre-brand banner and number plate,
and two images under 700 px.
