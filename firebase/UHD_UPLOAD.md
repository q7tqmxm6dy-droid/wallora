# Uploading the 4K pack

`uhd-upload/` (git-ignored, ~21 MB) holds genuine **2160 × 3840** versions: the photos that have a real 4K source, and the original Luxury patterns, which are drawn directly at 4K. Nothing is upscaled.

## How it works in the app

- The 1080p copies stay in the app as the offline fallback.
- When you upload a 4K file with the **same title and category** as a bundled wallpaper, the cloud copy replaces the bundled one (no duplicate), keeps its photo credit and license, and gets a **4K** badge.
- Uploads are flagged `uhd` automatically (the admin page reads the image size). A **4K Ultra HD** filter chip appears on Home once at least one 4K wallpaper exists.

## Steps

1. Open the admin page, sign in, choose **Upload a file**.
2. For each row below: pick the file, type the **exact title**, choose the **exact category**, upload.
3. Open the app: the wallpaper now shows the 4K badge, and Home shows the 4K filter.
