Delete your Wallora account

Wallora works without an account. Only people who chose Sign in with Google have one.

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open Wallora and go to Profile.
  2. In the account card, tap Delete account and confirm.

If the app asks you to sign in again first, do that and repeat the steps. This is a safety check by Google.

If you can no longer open the app

Email ahmad.qiblawi112@gmail.com from the Google account you signed in with, with the subject “Delete my Wallora account”. We delete the account and its data within 30 days and confirm by reply.

What is deleted: your account, your email address, name and profile picture as stored by Wallora, and the cloud copy of your favourite wallpapers and albums. What stays: favourites and albums already on your phone (remove the app to erase them) and anonymous usage and crash statistics, which are not linked to your account.
