Wallora ● Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 September 2026

Short version: Wallora does not ask you to create an account, does not collect your name, email, location, contacts or photos, and does not sell anything to anyone. Your favourites, collections and settings stay on your device.

1. Information stored on your device

The following is saved locally on your phone and never leaves it:

  • wallpapers you mark as favourites
  • collections you create
  • your theme choice, daily-rotation and live-wallpaper settings
  • a short list of recently viewed wallpapers and your usage streak

Uninstalling the app removes all of it.

2. Information processed by our service

Wallpapers in the cloud catalogue are stored with Google Firebase. When the app opens it reads that public catalogue (a list of titles, categories and image links). This request does not include any personal information about you.

3. Diagnostics and usage statistics

The app uses Google Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics, which may collect:

  • anonymous usage events, such as “wallpaper set”, “wallpaper saved”, “wallpaper shared”, “favourite toggled” and which wallpaper was opened (by ID)
  • crash reports, including device model, Android version and a stack trace
  • a random app-instance identifier

These are used only to understand which features are used and to fix crashes. They are not used to identify you personally and are not sold or shared for advertising.

4. What we never collect

  • your name, email address or phone number (unless you are the publisher signing in to the admin area — see below)
  • your location
  • your contacts, calendar, messages or call history
  • your photo library

5. Images you share into the app

If another app shares an image with Wallora, that image is read only to set it as your wallpaper. It is never uploaded anywhere.

6. Publisher / admin sign-in

The app contains a hidden administrator area, used only by the person who publishes wallpapers. Signing in uses Firebase Authentication with an email address and password, which is stored by Google Firebase. Ordinary users never sign in and never provide an email address.

7. Third-party services

Data described above is processed by Google Firebase. See Firebase’s privacy information and the Google Privacy Policy.

8. Data retention and deletion

Analytics and crash data are retained by Google Firebase according to the retention settings of the Wallora Firebase project. Because the app has no user accounts, there is no personal profile to delete. Removing the app deletes all locally stored data.

9. Children

Wallora is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them.

10. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the “last updated” date above will change and the new version will be published at this address.
